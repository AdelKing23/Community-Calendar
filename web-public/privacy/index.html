Coast Calendar

Privacy

Coast Calendar is a local listings app and website for events, activities and community notices.

We use account and listing information to operate accounts, receive submissions, review listings, provide support, keep the platform safe, and support member subscriptions where available.

Payments are handled by Apple or the payment provider shown in the app. Coast Calendar does not store card details. We do not sell personal information.

You can request account deletion from Account details in the iPhone app. We delete account and personal data we are not legally required to keep. Apple subscriptions must be cancelled with Apple separately.

Public listings and published host pages are visible to anyone. Contact details appear only when the host or listing includes them.

Questions: use Support or email the address listed in App Store Connect.
